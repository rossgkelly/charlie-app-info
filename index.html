Charlie Home AI

Charlie Home AI is a privately operated personal assistant for its owner and invited household members. Its Google Calendar integration supports calendar summaries, scheduling assistance, and calendar changes requested by authorized users.

Google access is granted through Google's OAuth consent flow. Charlie is not a public sign-up service. This informational page provides no access to the assistant, its accounts, or calendar data.
